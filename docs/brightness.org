#+TITLE: Brightness: who sets it, what went wrong, what changed
#+DATE: 2026-09-27

Reported: random brightness changes, flashes, and the panel at a different
brightness than chosen.  Everything here comes from reading the scripts, the
Mahogany idle code and the kernel sources; the laptop was off, so nothing is
installed or measured yet (see the test plan at the end).  Branch =brightness=
here and in lambda-symbolics/mahogany.

* Who writes the backlight level

The intended owner is =userland/brightness=.  It writes
=hw.acpi.acpiout15.brightness= and =acpiout16=, which evaluate the panel's
=_BCM=; the firmware passes the level to i915 through the opregion (ASLE),
and i915 sets the PWM.  AC and battery levels are remembered separately in
=/home/mag/.brightness.{ac,bat}=.  Its callers: the powerd brightness keys
(+10/-10), =acadapter= (restore for the new supply), =lid_switch=, =s0ix=
(save before the freeze, restore after), Mahogany's idle timer (dim after two
minutes, restore on input) and the session start.

Other writers the scripts did not account for:

- *i915 itself*, whenever it turns the backlight on (a panel power-on after a
  modeset, resume, an output enabled again).  It uses its cached level, and a
  cached level at or below the panel minimum becomes the maximum
  (=__intel_panel_enable_backlight=, intel_panel.c).
- *The firmware's LPS0 display-off =_DSM=* (s2idle entry) drops its level to
  the minimum, and display-on does not put it back (seen on 9/19: both outputs
  read 1 after a resume).
- *thinkpad(4)*: on every brightness key it asks the BIOS to step the level
  through the CMOS interface (=\UCMS=), then sends the PSWITCH event that runs
  the powerd action.  Under genfb that BIOS step was a no-op (9/16); with
  i915's opregion it can land now.  Unverified.
- *acpiout*: if the firmware sends the video output 0x86/0x87 notifications,
  acpidisp steps the level by 5 on its own.  Unverified.

* What was wrong

1. *A full-brightness flash at every resume.*  =s0ix suspend= set both levels
   to 0 before freezing.  i915 cached that as its minimum, and on resume it lit
   the panel at full brightness until =s0ix= restored the level after
   userspace thawed.  Zeroing was never needed with i915: the freeze suspends
   i915drmkms, which turns the panel off.  Fixed: =s0ix= remembers the level
   (the firmware's display-off call still lowers it) but no longer zeroes it.
2. *Steps from a dimmed panel became the preference.*  After two idle minutes
   the panel dims to 15.  The brightness keys arrive through ACPI, not wskbd,
   so they did not count as input and did not undim; one press gave 15 + 10 =
   25 and saved 25 as the level for the supply.  The next undim, AC change or
   lid open then brought back a panel darker than chosen.  Fixed: steps start
   from the remembered level, never from what the panel shows, and the keys
   call Mahogany's new =idle-poke=, which undims and restarts the idle deadlines.
3. *Back from blanking at the dim level.*  Dim at 2 min, blank at 5, then
   input: the restore was spawned before the output came back on, and could
   run while i915 was asleep.  The panel then came on at 15 and jumped, or
   stayed dim.  Fixed: blanking puts the remembered level back while the panel
   is off (i915 keeps it for the power-on), and unblanking asserts it once more
   with the panel on.
4. *Possibly two or three owners per key press* (the BIOS step and the acpiout
   step next to the powerd action).  Kernel 131 adds switches and counters
   for both; =brightness-probe= tells which ones act.
5. The lid-nosuspend path zeroed the level as well, with the same flash as 1
   when Mahogany turned the output back on.  It now leaves the backlight to
   Mahogany when Mahogany runs (X still needs the zero).

Working as designed, but easy to mistake for randomness: plugging in or
unplugging changes the brightness (separate AC and battery levels), and the
panel dims after two minutes without keyboard or pointer input.

* The 30 Hz mode

No brightness setting is tied to the refresh rate.  A seamless switch changes
only the link M/N; the backlight PWM and i915's level are not touched.  But
since the Mahogany switch (9/26) the kernel's idleness DRRS drops the panel to
30 Hz one second after the last frame and returns to 60 Hz on the next, so in
normal use it switches many times a minute.  That can show in two ways:

- the panel's own luminance can differ at 30 Hz (an LCD holds each frame
  twice as long), which would look like the brightness changing a second
  after the screen goes still;
- a glitch at the switch: idleness DRRS rewrites the four M/N registers from
  a work item without vblank evasion (Mahogany's explicit switch does evade),
  so a switch that straddles the start of vblank can show one bad frame.

Kernel 131 adds =hw.drm2.i915_modparams.lispbsd_idle_drrs= (default 1) to turn
idleness DRRS off at runtime; =s-R= turns off Mahogany's 30 fps matching.  If
the flashes stop with both off, it is the switching.  Then either keep idleness
DRRS off, or do its M/N write under vblank evasion and look again.  The power
the 30 Hz drop saves has not been measured on its own; measure it in the same
A/B.

* Changes (branch =brightness=)

- =userland/brightness=: the remembered level is the truth.  New =dim N= and
  =undim= for the idle timer (dim marks the panel dimmed in
  =/var/run/user/1000/brightness.dimmed= and leaves the remembered level
  alone); a restore while dimmed leaves the panel dimmed; one =sysctl= call
  for both outputs; a lock, since powerd and the compositor can run it at once.
- =userland/s0ix=: no zeroing before the freeze.
- =etc/powerd/actions/brightness-{up,down}=: poke Mahogany; skip while
  =/var/run/brightness.hold= exists (for the probe).
- =etc/powerd/scripts/lid_switch=: no zeroing when Mahogany runs.
- Mahogany =lisp/idle.lisp=: dim, undim and restore through the script; undim
  while blanked, restore after unblanking; =idle-poke=.
- Kernel 131 (prepared, not built): =hw.acpi.acpiout{15,16}.hotkeys= (0: the
  firmware's brightness notifications no longer step the level) with a
  "brightness notify" event counter per output; =hw.acpi.thinkpad0.bios_brightness=
  (0: no BIOS step on the keys) with a "brightness hotkey" counter;
  =lispbsd_idle_drrs=.  All default to the old behaviour.  =acpi_display.c= and
  =thinkpad_acpi.c= are the 11.0 release files; diff them against the laptop's
  =/usr/src/sys= before installing.
- =userland/brightness-probe=: samples both levels every 50 ms while you press
  a key, with the powerd actions held off (or running, with =-a=), and prints
  the kernel counters.

* Test plan

1. Install kernel 131, the scripts and Mahogany's =idle.lisp= (hot-load with
   =mahoganyctl '(load ...)'=).
2. =brightness-probe=, press up twice.  Any change means a kernel or firmware
   owner: a "brightness notify" count means acpiout, so set
   =hw.acpi.acpiout15.hotkeys=0= and =acpiout16=; a change with only the
   thinkpad0 count means the BIOS step, so set =hw.acpi.thinkpad0.bios_brightness=0=.
   Put what was needed in =etc/sysctl.conf=.
3. =brightness-probe -a=: one press must give exactly one change of 10.
4. Lid shut and open on battery: no flash when it opens.
5. Wait 2 minutes (dim), press brightness up: the remembered level + 10, not
   25.  Wait 5 minutes (blank), touch a key: the panel comes back at the
   remembered level without a step.
6. The 30 Hz A/B above, if flashes remain.
