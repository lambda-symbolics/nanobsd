#+title: Crash-path audit of 2026-10-30: what was done

An external review of the kernel changes since 4e863a5
(nanobsd-crash-audit-2026-10-30.zip: REVIEW.md, host-only control-flow
models).  Each finding was checked against the code; all six are real.
Kernel 216 carried the first fixes; kernel 220 (default since 2026-10-30)
finishes every item, including the ones 216 had left alone, and each
failure path was exercised on the laptop with an injection knob (below).  None of them is a proven cause of the
post-resume corruption dumps (21-23); see [[file:post-resume-corruption.org]].

* A. xHCI: ring freed after an unacknowledged stop and drop
Confirmed.  =xhci_close_pipe= ignored the result of both STOP_ENDPOINT and
the dropping CONFIGURE_ENDPOINT and freed the ring anyway;
=xhci_event_transfer= asserted (or, without DIAGNOSTIC, dereferenced) a
NULL ring for an event that arrived after the close.

Fixed (bounded):
- close records whether the controller is known to be done with the ring
  (endpoint not running, or the stop acknowledged); if it is not and the
  drop fails too, the ring is leaked instead of freed (its cookies were
  already cleared) and the kernel says "did not stop; keeping its ring";
- the event handler drops events with an out-of-range slot (the slot field
  is 8 bits, the array is sc_maxslots + 1) and events for a slot or
  endpoint with no ring.

Kernel 220 also covers the abort path: =xhci_pipe_restart= resets an
endpoint that halted under the stop, retries a failed stop once, and
returns whether the endpoint ended up stopped (or in error, or disabled).
If not, =xhci_abortx= marks every transfer queued on the pipe =ux_dmaleak=
(new field in usbdivar.h) and =usbd_free_buffer= leaks such a buffer
instead of freeing it ("usb: leaking N-byte transfer buffer ...").
=xhci_set_dequeue= now returns its status.

Test knob: =hw.usbpm.inject_stopfail=N= reports the next N endpoint stops
(abort or close) as unconfirmed after really stopping them.  Verified on the
camera: three injected abort failures leaked its three 40 KB isochronous
buffers, an injected close failure kept the ring ("did not stop (15);
keeping its ring"), and the camera streamed normally afterwards.

* B. i915: OA unit TLB not invalidated on Tiger Lake
Confirmed backport gap.  Kernel 211 ported Linux 7938d61591d3 (engine TLB
flush before pages are released) but not its follow-up 180abeb2c503
(Wa_2207587034): on gen12 the OA unit has its own TLB.  Kernel 216 writes 1
to GEN12_OA_TLB_INV_CR (0xceec) after the engine invalidations, under the
uncore lock, on Tiger Lake (the only one of the upstream platforms this
i915 knows).

* C. i915: a timed-out invalidation still lets the pages go
Confirmed (upstream behaves the same).  Kernel 220: =intel_gt_invalidate_tlbs=
returns whether every engine confirmed; if not,
=__i915_gem_object_unset_pages= runs =intel_gt_reset_full= (new, intel_reset.c)
before the pages are handed back: a full GT reset, which drops every TLB,
with no engine blamed, so requests in flight are replayed.  It is the
full-reset half of =intel_gt_handle_error= (no per-engine attempt) and waits
out a reset already in progress before doing its own.

Test knob: =hw.i915tlbfail=N= treats the next N invalidations as timed out.

How it got there:
- kernel 218 wedged and unwedged the GT instead; the engines were left
  uninitialised and the next heartbeat request faulted in
  =__i915_request_create= (dump 24);
- kernel 219 used the full reset, which livelocked a CPU: the reset
  disables the execlists tasklets, and NetBSD's Linux tasklet emulation
  requeued a disabled tasklet and retriggered its softint until it was
  enabled again.  The thread that would enable it was the one the softint
  had interrupted on that CPU: "heart stopped beating" in softser (dump 25).
  Linux escapes the same pattern through ksoftirqd; NetBSD softints have no
  such fallback.  Kernel 220 parks a disabled tasklet off the queue and the
  enable that brings the count to zero requeues it
  (external/bsd/common/linux/linux_tasklet.c, PARKED and HI state bits).
- kernel 220: one injected failure, then three in a row, each "Resetting
  chip", every encode job finished, the GPU worked afterwards.  One frame of
  one job got EIO during the back-to-back resets.

* D. SOF: stream buffers freed without a confirmed stop
Confirmed.  =stream_teardown= cleared RUN and freed the buffers at once.
Now =stream_stop= clears RUN and the interrupt enables, polls RUN until it
reads 0, then resets the stream checking both SRST edges (Linux
=hda_dsp_stream_trigger= STOP and =hda_dsp_stream_reset=).  If that fails
the buffers are leaked, not freed; the stream is always given back to
hdaudio so no interrupt can reach the module after unload.  Verified with
modload on the laptop: both streams stop and reset, buffers freed.

INTCTL (kernel 220): every update goes through =hdaudio_intctl_update=, a
read-modify-write under a new leaf spin lock (=sc_intctl_mtx=, IPL_HIGH),
used by hdaudio's stream start/stop, interrupt enable/disable and reset,
and by sofdsp.  Verified: sofdsp loaded during playback, playback took its
real time and the module's streams stopped cleanly.

* E. SOF: FW_READY mailbox parsing bounds
Confirmed.  Now:
- =mbox_read= refuses reads that are not word-aligned or leave the 4 KB
  uplink mailbox (subtraction-form check);
- each extended record must be word-sized and fit in the mailbox, and a
  window record must hold at least =struct sof_ipc_window= before its
  count is read; the count is capped by the record size;
- window id, offset and size are checked against the four 128 KB SRAM
  windows and the BAR before being kept as MMIO offsets;
- the HDA capability walk is capped at 16 entries inside the HDA BAR;
- the DSP BAR must cover all four windows.

* F. uvideo bulk: stop waited before cancelling, ignored signals
Confirmed (bulk cameras only; the Nano's camera is isochronous).  Stop now
clears =bx_running=, suspends the pipe (cancels the worker's synchronous
transfer and refuses new ones, so the worker cannot slip one in after its
check), then =kthread_join=s the worker (created KTHREAD_MUSTJOIN) before
destroying the transfer.  Also fixed: the start path returned the wrong
variable when kthread_create failed.

* Screened-out lead: payload header longer than the packet
=uvideo_stream_recv_process= now rejects bHeaderLength > len; video(4)'s
size check already stopped the underflowed length from being copied.
