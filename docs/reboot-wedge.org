#+TITLE: Reboots that wedge in the final shutdown phase
#+DATE: 2026-10-02

* Symptom

Some reboots on kernel 131 (perhaps earlier) never finish: the screen goes
dark, one core spins, the fans run until the power button is held.

* What the logs show

Every wedged shutdown gets through =rc.shutdown=: syslogd logs "Exiting on
signal 15", and the next boot follows one to three hours later, where a
clean reboot takes 35-40 s (Oct 1 10:34 -> 11:56, on battery; Oct 2 09:45 ->
11:03, 10 s after the charger was connected).  One stop on Oct 2 left the
tail of =/var/log/messages= filled with stale blocks of a Firefox =prefs.js=:
the WAPBL journal covers metadata only, so a file that grew just before an
unclean stop shows old disk contents.

The hang therefore sits in the kernel's last phase.  =cpu_reboot()= on amd64
loops

#+begin_src c
while (vfs_unmountall1(curlwp, false, false) ||
       config_detach_all(boothowto) ||
       vfs_unmount_forceone(curlwp))
        ;
#+end_src

until neither an unmount nor a detach makes progress.  A detach or unmount
that reports success forever spins here after the display is already gone,
which matches "screen off, hot, fans".  Ruled out: =nvme_shutdown()= (bounded
at 4 s), =hw.acpi.sleep.idle_d3= (off), gefs (not mounted).

Four test reboots on 2026-10-02 were clean: fresh boot on battery, after a
30 s timed s2idle, 20 s after plugging the charger in, and a plain one.

* The safety net

=etc/rc.shutdown.final= (sourced at the end of =rc.shutdown=, after every
rc.d script has stopped) arms the software watchdog for 180 s with nobody to
tickle it.  A shutdown still running then panics (=hw.swwdog.reboot=0=), and
with =ddb.onpanic=0= and =ddb.dumpstack=1= the backtrace goes to the message
buffer, which survives the warm reboot.  After the next wedge, read the end
of the previous boot in =dmesg=; =/var/tmp/rc.shutdown.final.ran= records the
last arming.  =wdogctl -x= is the mode that stays armed when wdogctl exits.

* 2026-10-21: why the safety net stayed silent, and its replacement (kernel 192)
Astra's review of the swwdog arming: =wdogctl -x= forks a tickler (=-e=
arms and exits); swwdog's callout is not MPSAFE, so a driver callback stuck
in the shutdown walk with the kernel lock held also holds the watchdog off;
and a watchdog panic from the callout's LWP waits at kern_reboot's rebooter
gate instead of rebooting.  The 10-21 wedge (on AC, >20 min dark) proves
nothing about the watchdog having fired.  A TCO hardware watchdog would be
independent, but NetBSD's ichsmb only drives the TCO on 100-series PCHs.

Kernel 192 watches the shutdown itself:
- =lispbsd_shutdown_mark()= (kern_reboot.c) names each step before it
  runs: kern_reboot, sync, every unmount and forced unmount, every detach,
  PMF driver and bus shutdown per device, the shutdown hooks one by one
  (by address), S5, the halt of the other CPUs.  Each mark is printed,
  recorded, written back to RAM (=wbinvd=: a reset discards dirty cache
  lines, and without it the newest lines, the ones that matter, were
  lost), and restarts a no-progress deadline (=kern.lispbsd_shutdown_timeout=,
  default 60 s, TSC-based so it does not depend on the primary CPU's tick).
- Every CPU checks the deadline in hardclock (cidle keeps the ticks
  periodic once =shutting_down= is set); the first to see it pass prints
  the stuck step and calls =cpu_reset()= directly: no callout, no kernel
  lock, no panic path.  The message buffer survives the warm reset.
- Halt (waiting for a key) disarms it.  After =cpu_broadcast_halt()= only
  the rebooting CPU runs, at splhigh: a hang past that point is not caught.
- =etc/rc.shutdown.final= arms swwdog with =-e= and records wdogctl's status.
- Validated 2026-10-21 with =kern.lispbsd_shutdown_test_spin=30= and a 10 s
  timeout: the next boot's dmesg ends with "shutdown watchdog: no progress
  for 10 s, stuck in: shutdown-hook 0xffffffff80d43f4e (noticed on cpu4);
  resetting".
- Seen on the way: a normal shutdown cannot unmount the gefs+ root and the
  loop falls through to =force-unmount /= (which succeeds).

After the next wedge: warm reset by the deadline, then
=dmesg | grep -E 'shutdown:|shutdown watchdog'= names the step.
