#+TITLE: TGL SAGV/QGV on the Nano (kernel 180)

Backport and reviews: pending/sagv/ (README.org, REVIEW.org); in kernel/
since 9e35b2b.  Switch: hw.drm2.i915_modparams.lispbsd_sagv (0 stock, 1
driver-managed SAGV, 2 pinned at the top point), now 1 in /etc/sysctl.conf.

* Firmware facts (boot log)
pcode global info 0x443: LPDDR4, 4 channels, 4 QGV points, DCLK 2134 / 3201
/ 4268 / 4268 MHz (13.3 / 18.5 / 23.0 / 23.0 GB/s for one plane), SAGV block
time 11 us.  SA_PERF_STATUS low byte: 0x20 = lowest point, 0x40 = top
(confirmed by pinning: mode 2 reads 0x40 throughout).

* Behaviour (2026-10-17)
- Fresh boot, mode 0: 0x20 at idle (load not sampled).
- Mode 1: low at idle and through a looping video; briefly top during heavy
  scrolling.  2876+ commits with no underrun or WM mismatch; Firefox scroll,
  video and the seamless 60/30 Hz switch visually clean (user).
- Leaving 1/2 for 0 keeps the top point pinned (by design) until reboot.

* Energy, video looping on battery (=userland/wakecensus/sagv-ab.sh 300=)
| mode | rate sensor | Wh counter | memory |
| 1    | 6.45 W      | 6.24 W     | low    |
| 2    | 7.26 W      | 7.20 W     | top    |
| 2    | 7.43 W      | 7.44 W     | top    |
| 1    | 6.57 W      | 6.84 W     | low    |
Pinned top costs ~0.8 W (12 %) under video.  Open: whether the firmware's
own mode-0 behaviour stays low under load (then part of this is not new).
