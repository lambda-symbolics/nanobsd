#+TITLE: 802.11n on the Nano's AX201 (iwx)
#+DATE: 2026-10-22

* What it is
NetBSD 11's net80211 only knows 11a/b/g, and our iwx(4) port had OpenBSD's
HT code stripped.  Kernels 193-198 put 802.11n station mode back, modeled on
OpenBSD:

- net80211 (=kernel/net80211=): HT Capabilities/Operation parsing in beacons
  and the association response, our HT Capabilities element in the
  (re)association request, HT negotiation (=ieee80211_ht_negotiate=, CCMP or
  open only), Block Ack action frames (ADDBA request/response, DELBA), the
  transmit agreement state with backoff, A-MSDU decapsulation, and a separate
  non-QoS sequence counter (index 16; it used to share TID 0's, which would
  punch holes into a TID 0 Block Ack window).
- iwx: HT rates and firmware rate control (MCS 0-15, SGI, STBC), 40 MHz PHY
  context, HT station flags and protection, the firmware Rx Block Ack session
  and the driver reorder buffer (with a 100 ms gap timer, as Linux), BAR and
  firmware frame-release notifications, Tx aggregation queues and compressed
  Block Ack reclaim.

* The switch
=hw.iwx.ht= (0/1) decides what the *next* association offers; it is 1 in
=/etc/sysctl.conf=.  To go back: =sysctl -w hw.iwx.ht=0; wpa_cli -i iwx0
reassociate=.  HT also turns WME (QoS) on, as an HT station must be a QoS
station.  =hw.iwx.ht_status= shows the association (width, SM power save,
AP MCS), Rx Block Ack sessions, reorder counters, the last Rx/Tx
=rate_n_flags= (0x0010ca17 = HT MCS 15, 40 MHz, SGI) and Tx aggregation.

* Tx aggregation is the firmware's job
The AX201 firmware sends the ADDBA request and consumes the AP's response
itself once the driver binds an aggregation queue to the TID (OpenBSD's
IEEE80211_C_ADDBA_OFFLOAD; =ic_addba_offload= here).  Requests sent by
net80211 were acked by the AP but never answered (kernel 197 probe), and the
answers never reached the driver.  So net80211 just calls
=ic_ampdu_tx_start= when a TID carries QoS data.

* SM power save
With =powersave= on, the association request asks for dynamic SM power save
and the PHY context listens on one chain, the second waking on the AP's RTS
(Linux's default).  Same download rate as with both chains always on.

* Measured (2026-10-22, Zyxel 5 GHz ch 40, 40 MHz below, LAN peer on Wi-Fi)
| direction | legacy 11a | HT, kernel 194 (Rx only) | HT, kernel 198 |
|-----------+------------+--------------------------+----------------|
| download  | 13 Mbit/s  | 39-45 Mbit/s             | 100-112 Mbit/s |
| upload    | 12.6       | 16-17                    | 100            |

* Not done
- Idle and transfer power not measured yet (fewer airtime-seconds per byte
  should help; two chains during MIMO bursts cost some).
- s2idle with HT associated is untested.
- No VHT (11ac) or HE (11ax).
